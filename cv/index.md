## Funded research

Approximately CAD 3M in competitive funding from federal and industry sources.

- NSERC CREATE, “Sustainable Data Systems for Data Science.” Theme lead and co-investigator, 2024 to 2030.
- NSERC Discovery Grant, “Systems and Algorithms for Integrating LLMs and GNNs into Data Science and Knowledge Graph Platforms.” PI, 2025 to 2030. Previously “Systems and Algorithms for Easy-to-use Federated Data Science,” 2020 to 2025.
- NRC Canada, “Cybersecurity Framework for the Digitized Construction Industry.” Co-investigator, 2025 to 2028.
- Concordia University Faculty Research Support Funds, PI, 2020 to 2027. MITACS partnerships with Qohash, National Bank of Canada and RBC Borealis AI, PI, 2022 to 2024.
